{%- comment -%} Sets `role_title` to the title of the role `include.role`, made from its name: each word between `_` with its first letter capitalized (`visiting_scholar` becomes "Visiting Scholar"), or the role as it is when that leaves nothing. Used for the People page's groups that `site.people_groups` does not title and for a person's earlier roles. It prints nothing: the caller escapes `role_title` where it prints it. Variables are prefixed `role_title`: an include shares its caller's variables. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign role_title_words = include.role | split: "_" -%} {%- assign role_title = "" -%} {%- for role_title_w in role_title_words -%} {%- assign role_title_first = role_title_w | slice: 0 | upcase -%} {%- assign role_title_rest = role_title_w | slice: 1, role_title_w.size -%} {%- assign role_title = role_title | append: " " | append: role_title_first | append: role_title_rest -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign role_title = role_title | strip | default: include.role -%}
