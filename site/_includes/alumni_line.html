{%- comment -%} The alumni line of `include.person`, escaped, made from its structured fields only, never from `bio` (README, "Rendering rules"). A string that is null or only whitespace is missing; a present one is trimmed. - when: with a degree, " ", or "" with no end_year; otherwise the years as year_range.html gives them: "–" ("" when they are equal), "from ", "until ", or nothing. - line: ", now ", "Now " with no when, "" with no current_position, or empty when both are missing.
